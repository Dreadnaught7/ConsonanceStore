# Consonance Publishing Pricing Standard

## Reader-first pricing

Consonance Publishing uses reader-first pricing for consumer editions. The goal is to keep entry prices accessible while preserving enough margin to cover production, payment processing, fulfillment overhead, and publishing operations.

Canonical reader-price targets are stored on each configured title as `readerPriceCents`.

## Institutional and bulk pricing

Libraries, universities, schools, museums, archives, nonprofit programs, booksellers, book clubs, and other institutional buyers use a separate quote-based lane. Volume, shipping, intended use, and fulfillment method may change the effective price.

## Synchronization rule

A public price change is not complete until the same effective sale price is reflected in:

1. the Consonance storefront;
2. the active payment processor;
3. the accounting product record in QuickBooks;
4. the fulfillment route or Lulu retail configuration when Lulu controls the transaction price.

Do not advertise one price and charge another.

## Accounting

QuickBooks is the accounting system of record for title-level revenue. Physical print-on-demand books are represented as non-inventory products unless Consonance begins stocking units directly. Production charges, payment-processing fees, shipping, refunds, and other transaction costs should be recorded separately so title margin can be measured from actual data.
