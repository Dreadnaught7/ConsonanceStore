# Consonance Publishing Interior QR Standard

**Effective immediately, every book must include a QR code inside the interior pages that directs readers to the Consonance Publishing catalog.**

## Purpose

Every Consonance title should give readers a direct path to discover the rest of the catalog, related books and series, and future releases.

## Placement

Place the QR code in the back matter, preferably on the final promotional or publisher page before the book ends.

The code should be large enough to scan reliably while remaining consistent with the book's design.

## Required destination

**Catalog:** https://consonanceintelligence.com/store

A printed version of the catalog URL must appear beneath or beside the QR code for readers who cannot or prefer not to scan QR codes.

## Production requirements

Before a title is approved for publication:

1. Confirm the QR code points to the catalog URL above.
2. Test the QR code from the final print-ready PDF, not only from the design source.
3. Confirm the printed URL is visible and accurate.
4. Confirm the QR code remains readable at the final trim size and is not placed inside the bleed, gutter, or unsafe margin.

## Scope

This standard applies to:

- all new Consonance Publishing books;
- all revised or new editions when interior files are updated;
- replacement print files prepared for Lulu or another print/distribution platform.

A title is not production-complete until this check passes.
