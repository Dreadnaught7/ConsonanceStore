# Institutional and Bulk Pricing Standard

Consonance Publishing keeps consumer reader pricing separate from institutional and bulk-order pricing.

## Default volume framework

The following is the internal starting framework for physical-book quotes. It is a ceiling, not an automatic entitlement: every quote must remain above the title's landed print/fulfillment floor.

| Quantity | Default discount from reader price |
| --- | ---: |
| 1-4 copies | Reader price |
| 5-9 copies | Up to 5% |
| 10-24 copies | Up to 10% |
| 25-49 copies | Up to 15% |
| 50+ copies | Custom quote |

Shipping is quoted separately unless the written quote explicitly includes it.

## Buyer lanes

- Libraries and archives: reader price for ordinary single-copy acquisition; volume pricing for multi-copy or program orders.
- Faculty and classrooms: desk/review copies may be digital and selective; classroom orders use the volume framework.
- Museums, nonprofits, cultural organizations and book clubs: volume framework.
- Booksellers/resellers: custom wholesale quote. Do not treat reseller orders as ordinary institutional discounts because resale margin requirements are different.
- Rights/licensing: separate negotiated lane.

## Margin floor

No bulk quote may be issued until the title's current print cost and expected fulfillment cost are known. The quoted unit price must cover:

1. print production;
2. payment-processing cost when applicable;
3. fulfillment/handling overhead;
4. any shipping amount absorbed by Consonance;
5. a positive publishing margin.

## Quote discipline

Quotes should state title, format, quantity, unit price, shipping treatment, tax treatment, quote expiration, and fulfillment route.

Consumer storefront prices should not be changed to accommodate one institutional order.
