# Bundle and Institutional Pricing Framework

## Status

This framework is operational guidance only until title-level production costs are verified. Do not advertise a discount that pushes a physical title below its approved margin floor.

## Consumer bundles

Planned bundle families:

- WHO ARE WE? — Books One + Two
- Before the Bullet — Target: Black Messiah + A Dream Observed + The Means They Feared
- The Seven Suns of the V’Nari — complete trilogy
- GROUNDS — Harlem + Bed-Stuy
- Consonance Fiction — curated multi-title set

Bundle rule: calculate the bundle from the active reader prices, then apply a modest bundle discount only after print cost, Square fee, shipping treatment, and fulfillment margin are confirmed.

## Institutional lane

Libraries, schools, universities, museums, archives, cultural organizations, nonprofits, book clubs, and other institutional buyers remain quote-based.

Working volume bands:

- 1–4 copies: active reader price
- 5–9 copies: up to 10% discount when margin permits
- 10–24 copies: up to 15% discount when margin permits
- 25+ copies: custom quote
- Booksellers / resale: custom wholesale quote based on title economics and order size

Shipping is quoted separately unless a written quote explicitly includes it.

## Controls

Before issuing a quote:

1. confirm the title's current reader price;
2. confirm current print / fulfillment cost;
3. confirm quantity and destination;
4. include expected payment-processing cost where applicable;
5. preserve a positive title margin;
6. record the quoted price and buyer type for later analysis.

Institutional discounts are not automatic public coupons.
